# Final QA Checklist

## Content

- All names, dates, figures, titles, and terminology match the source brief.
- Simulated values are marked.
- Placeholder text is removed or intentionally retained.
- No unsupported claims are presented as facts.

## Layout

- Page count is expected.
- No blank overflow pages.
- No clipped, overlapping, or hidden text.
- Headings do not orphan at page bottoms.
- No single-character title wraps.
- Mixed orientations are intentional.
- Margins and baselines are consistent.

## Images

- Images are sharp at final size.
- No distortion.
- No excessive internal whitespace.
- Brand imagery is not reused across distinct brands without approval.
- Captions and alt text are present where needed.

## Tables

- Native and editable when appropriate.
- Header rows repeat where needed.
- Numeric alignment is consistent.
- Text remains readable at normal zoom.

## Navigation and metadata

- Heading styles are real.
- TOC and page numbers are correct.
- Headers/footers are appropriate by page type.
- Comments and tracked changes are removed unless requested.
- Personal metadata is scrubbed when needed.

## PDF

- Every page renders.
- Page dimensions are correct.
- Fonts and glyphs render correctly.
- Forms, links, and annotations work when relevant.
