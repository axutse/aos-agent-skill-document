# TAIZHOU Brand Enterprise White Paper — Page Blueprint

## Recommended full edition: 100–130 pages

### Front matter — 4–6 pages

1. Cover
2. Core thesis
3. Publication information and data labels
4. Contents
5. White-paper master map
6. Six executive conclusions

### Chapter 1 — Enterprise, culture, organization, and governance — 20–28 pages

- About TAIZHOU
- Corporate philosophy
- Corporate culture in three pages: thesis, six values, operating translation
- Two companies and three brands
- Leadership and strategic decision layer
- Shared function centers
- Employee organization and reporting
- Company governance, brand governance, and brand operation boundaries
- Decision rights, RACI, meeting rhythm, conflict arbitration
- Brand asset ownership, contracts, funds, and data flows

### Chapter 2 — Multi-brand strategy — 15–20 pages

- Why three brands
- Brand portfolio map
- User and life-stage map
- Price and value matrix
- Lifestyle and scenario matrix
- Shared capability vs independent expression
- WANMIAN positioning, DNA, language, and growth flywheel
- GEERNA positioning, DNA, language, and value flywheel
- UIUP positioning, DNA, language, and content-growth flywheel
- Internal brand firewall

### Chapter 3 — Product and material planning — 14–20 pages

- Product operating system
- User insight to market launch
- Six-dimensional product review
- Six-category product portfolio
- Product-value pyramid
- Four material technology platforms
- Brand product split
- WANMIAN product direction
- GEERNA selection and second development
- UIUP rapid development loop
- Product lifecycle, launch review, inventory, and data feedback
- Representative product evidence pages

### Chapter 4 — Brand visual systems — 22–32 pages

- TAIZHOU corporate visual philosophy
- Corporate logo, typography, color, business card, and applications
- Three-brand visual relationship
- WANMIAN emotion page, rules, photography, detail page, packaging
- GEERNA emotion page, rules, photography, editorial, packaging, GN system
- UIUP emotion page, logo, rules, dynamic content, detail page, packaging and hangtag
- Cross-brand photography, packaging, retail, and asset-governance comparison

### Chapter 5 — Brand operation and media — 10–16 pages

- Brand operating models
- Channel system
- User journey and touchpoints
- Corporate, brand, sales, and internal media
- PESO media structure
- Content engine and publication approval
- Annual content topics and platform roles
- Media budget and performance measures
- Brand health dashboard

### Chapter 6 — Operating model and annual planning — 12–20 pages

- Company, brand, asset, contract, and cash relationships
- Annual product scale
- Price ladders
- Brand P&L model
- Sales targets
- Inventory, return rate, replenishment, and contribution-margin targets
- Brand asset lifecycle and official-version management

### Chapter 7 — Five-year direction — 4–8 pages

- 2026–2030 roadmap
- Capability ladder
- AI-enhanced brand operation
- Future TAIZHOU brand ecology
- Summary and back cover

## Compact edition: 60–80 pages

Merge governance details, user profiles, and application standards. Keep:

- 8–10 enterprise/governance pages
- 12–15 multi-brand pages
- 12–15 product/material pages
- 18–22 visual pages
- 8–10 operation/media pages
- 4–6 roadmap/summary pages
