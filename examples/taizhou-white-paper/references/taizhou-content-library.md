## 1. White-paper thesis

TAIZHOU is not a fourth consumer brand. It is the industrial, organizational, and governance system behind multiple consumer brands.

The two companies create and deliver capability. The three brands create user value and market results.

## 2. Enterprise positioning

TAIZHOU is a modern knitwear enterprise system connecting material research, product planning, apparel design, manufacturing, brand operation, and market sales.

The enterprise aims to move from manufacturing capability to product capability, from product capability to brand capability, and from brand capability to a continuously evolving brand ecology.

## 3. Corporate philosophy

Good clothing is not created by manufacturing alone. It is formed through material, design, process, manufacturing, and real human experience.

Material gives warmth. Design creates value. Manufacturing guarantees trust. Brand connects users.

## 4. Corporate culture

### Core value statement

Craftsmanship as the foundation. Technology as the wing. Quality as the soul.

### Integrity

Stay within clear boundaries, communicate honestly, and earn trust through truth and responsibility.

### Progress

Break through, continue learning, and redefine standards through competition and innovation.

### Collaboration

Build open cooperation, mutual trust, and shared responsibility across product, design, manufacturing, sales, and service.

### Innovation

Let technology carry warmth and let knitwear become a language. Explore new materials, processes, products, and experiences.

### Quality

Quality is the enterprise life line and the beginning of trust. Every process and every yarn carries the rigor and warmth of TAIZHOU manufacturing.

### Responsibility

Develop sustainably, reduce environmental impact, respect partners, and create products with longer-term meaning.

### Culture into operation

- Product: every material claim has evidence; every design has a purpose.
- Manufacturing: standards are clear and processes are traceable.
- Customer: promises are transparent and problems are not avoided.
- Organization: responsibility is clear and collaboration reduces internal friction.
- Partners: communicate fairly and pursue long-term mutual value.

## 5. Brand portfolio

### WANMIAN / 万棉尚品

Positioning: comfort lifestyle womenswear.

Core role: scale, repeat purchase, stable cash flow, foundational product capability.

Core words: comfort, skin-friendly, flattering, reliable, daily life.

Core statement: Let comfort become a value that can be felt every day.

Growth flywheel:

`comfortable product -> stable experience -> user trust -> repeat purchase -> scale growth`

### GEERNA / 哥尔纳

Positioning: minimalist premium womenswear.

Core role: material value, silhouette, brand premium, profit, and brand height.

Core words: material, silhouette, order, space, long-term wardrobe.

Core statement: Material determines touch. Silhouette determines attitude.

Value flywheel:

`premium material -> structural design -> brand perception -> price capability -> reinvestment`

### UIUP

Positioning: young body-focused city light-outdoor womenswear.

Core role: young users, content distribution, trend testing, and new growth.

Core words: body, movement, city, light functionality, youth, content energy.

Core statement: Body upward. Style forward.

Growth flywheel:

`trend content -> attention -> product experience -> social sharing -> brand growth`

## 6. Product portfolio

Default annual category structure:

- Base layers: 35%
- Knitted pants: 20%
- Sweatshirts: 15%
- Outerwear: 10%
- Thermal underwear: 10%
- Loungewear: 10%

### WANMIAN direction

- Skin-friendly base layers
- Comfortable straight, wide-leg, and relaxed knitted pants
- Light-warm sweatshirts
- Thin, non-restrictive thermal underwear
- Loungewear that can support short-distance outdoor use

### GEERNA direction

- Wool, cashmere, yak-cashmere, high-grade modal, and structured base layers
- Draped and architectural knitted pants
- Structured sweatshirts and knit tops
- Minimal jackets and coats
- Limited high-value image products

### UIUP direction

- Fitted, cropped, elastic base layers
- Flared and body-shaping knitted pants
- Cropped zip sweatshirts
- Light outerwear and light-outdoor sets
- Fast-testing content products

## 7. Material platforms

### Premium natural fiber platform

Yak cashmere, wool/cashmere blends, and other premium natural-fiber blends.

Purpose: brand height, high-value products, material storytelling.

### Stable wool platform

Anti-pilling wool, fine wool, light wool, and wool blends.

Purpose: texture, long-term wearing, stable size and quality.

### Thermal comfort platform

Thermal fabric, spacer fabric, wool spacer, modal spacer, and light-warm composites.

Purpose: autumn/winter scale, warmth, elasticity, structure.

### Skin-friendly base platform

Modal, lyocell, modal-wool, lyocell-wool, and elastic regenerated-fiber blends.

Purpose: skin feel, drape, cross-season use, repeat purchase.

## 8. Product review

Every product should pass six decisions:

1. User fit
2. Brand fit
3. Scenario fit
4. Price fit
5. Channel fit
6. Supply fit

A product should not enter mass production until it passes both market and production judgment.

## 9. Brand language

### WANMIAN voice

Warm, truthful, clear, reliable, not overly promotional.

Use: soft, comfortable, skin-friendly, flattering, everyday, reassuring.

Avoid: disruptive, luxury, permanent anti-pilling, miracle slimming.

### GEERNA voice

Rational, restrained, precise, quiet, opinionated.

Use: material, structure, proportion, touch, long-term wardrobe, order.

Avoid: viral must-buy, magic slimming, luxury-lady style, cheap alternative.

### UIUP voice

Direct, short, energetic, body-aware, content-friendly.

Use: body, movement, elasticity, proportion, city, upward.

Avoid: mature elegance, gentle-lady language, heavy outdoor jargon.
