# TAIZHOU Governance and Brand Architecture

## Core relationship

TAIZHOU is the enterprise and industrial capability system. The two companies carry legal responsibility, professional capability, cost, quality, and delivery. The three brands create differentiated consumer value and are evaluated as separate operating units.

## Default hierarchy

```text
Leadership and strategic decision layer
        ↓
TAIZHOU New Materials Technology Co., Ltd.
TAIZHOU Apparel Co., Ltd.
        ↓
Shared governance and professional capability platform
        ↓
WANMIAN | GEERNA | UIUP
        ↓
Product | Content | Channel | User | Service
        ↓
Sales, inventory, return, margin, and user data feedback
```

## Two-company responsibility split

### TAIZHOU New Materials Technology Co., Ltd.

- Material and fabric research
- Market and user research
- Product planning
- Apparel design and product development
- Brand strategy and visual asset management
- Sales, channels, media, and user data

### TAIZHOU Apparel Co., Ltd.

- Sample and pattern development
- Process standards
- Manufacturing
- Quality control and traceability
- Packaging, warehouse, fulfillment, and delivery
- Manufacturing cost and production efficiency

## Shared function centers

- Strategy and operations
- Product and material research
- Brand visual design
- Market and media
- Sales and customer
- Supply chain and manufacturing
- Finance and risk
- Data and systems
- Organization and talent

## Brand business units

Each business unit should include a lightweight core team:

- Brand lead
- Product planner
- Product designer
- Visual/content lead
- Channel operator
- User operator

Shared functions provide standards, resources, approval, and execution capacity. Brand business units own user, product, channel, inventory, and operating results.

## Governance boundaries

### Company governance

Controls organization, budget, financial risk, manufacturing efficiency, supply chain, data, and major investments.

### Brand governance

Controls positioning, product boundaries, visual assets, language, price, channel policy, and brand health.

### Brand operation

Owns daily product, content, sales, user, inventory, and P&L results.

## Default governance meetings

- Annual strategy and budget meeting
- Quarterly brand-business review
- Monthly product and operation meeting
- Weekly project execution review

## Brand conflict firewall

- No label-only cross-brand product conversion
- No shared hero model or hero scene
- No copied detail-page template
- Avoid major price-band overlap
- No long-term channel price war
- Cross-brand asset use requires approval

## Asset governance

Official assets include:

- Trademarks and logos
- Typography and color systems
- Product designs, patterns, BOM, and process files
- Photography, video, copy, detail pages, and packaging
- Domains, stores, social accounts, and user data

Every asset must have one official version, one owner, one approval path, one usage scope, and one archive location.
